Ranking Methodologies Explained

1. TrueSkill — Skill Estimation with Uncertainty

Calculates Bayesian skill estimate (mean μ) and uncertainty (variance σ) from 1v1 match results. The conservative display score μ - 3σ ensures unproven players with high uncertainty do not dominate the leaderboard.

2. Glicko-2 — Rating, Deviation & Volatility

Standard Glicko-2 model tracking skill rating r, rating deviation RD, and volatility σ. Inactivity increases uncertainty (RD) over time without arbitrarily reducing rating. Displayed conservative score is r - 2RD.

3. Tournament Placings — Results-Based Ranking

Awards points based on final tournament placements (1st=1000, 2nd=700, 3rd=500, etc.), scaled by entrant count multiplier. Directly reflects overall tournament finishes over the selected period.

4. Strength of Field — Field-Adjusted Performance

Evaluates placement relative to the competitive strength of event entrants, using pre-event ratings calculated prior to the tournament. Distinguishes deep regional brackets from casual beginner events.
