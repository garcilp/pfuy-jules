Ranking Methodologies

1. TrueSkill (Skill Estimation with Uncertainty)

Maintains skill mean (μ) and uncertainty (σ). Displayed score is conservative estimate (μ - 3σ). Higher uncertainty lowers the conservative rank until more matches are played.

2. Glicko-2 (Rating, Deviation, Volatility)

Standard rating algorithm tracking rating ($r$), rating deviation ($RD$), and volatility ($\sigma$). Displayed conservative score is $r - 2RD$. Inactivity increases $RD$.

3. Tournament Placings (Results-Based)

Awards placement points for event finishes scaled logarithmically by event entrant count ($1 + \log_{10}(\text{entrants})$). Evaluates total competitive accomplishments.

4. Strength of Field (Field-Adjusted Performance)

Evaluates placement points weighted by the pre-event TrueSkill ratings of fellow entrants, distinguishing equal placements in stacked tournaments versus smaller events.

Normalization in Custom Ranking Builder

Each system score is min-max normalized (0 to 100) across active players before applying user-selected weights.
